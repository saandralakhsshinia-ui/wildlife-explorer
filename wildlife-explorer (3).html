Through the Ages

Wildlife History

1800
Forests Everywhere

Earth's surface was blanketed by ancient old-growth forests. Wildlife populations thrived in undisturbed habitats spanning millions of hectares. Biodiversity was at its peak in modern history.

1950
Industrial Growth

Post-war industrialization accelerated deforestation and habitat destruction. Urban expansion fragmented wildlife corridors, isolating populations and triggering the first mass species decline of the modern era.

2000
Endangered Species

The IUCN Red List documented over 11,000 threatened species worldwide. Iconic animals — tigers, elephants, gorillas — faced critical population declines. Global awareness began to shift.

2024
Conservation Efforts

Nations pledged to protect 30% of land and ocean by 2030. Rewilding programs reintroduced apex predators. Technology-aided anti-poaching operations and genetic recovery programs show promising results.
